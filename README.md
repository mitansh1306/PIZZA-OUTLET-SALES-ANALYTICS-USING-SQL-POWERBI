# 🍕 Pizza Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Pizza Sales Performance Dashboard** built to analyze sales, orders, product performance, and customer ordering patterns. The dashboard provides a clear overview of key business metrics and helps identify top- and low-performing pizzas, revenue trends, and sales patterns.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

![Pizza Sales Performance Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform pizza sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that helps answer questions such as:

* How much total revenue was generated?
* How many orders and pizzas were sold?
* Which pizzas generate the most revenue?
* Which pizzas are ordered the most?
* Which pizza has the highest quantity sold?
* How does revenue change month-to-month?
* Which day of the week receives the most orders?
* Which pizza categories and sizes contribute most to sales?

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### Revenue & Product Performance

* **The Thai Chicken Pizza** is the top pizza by revenue.
* **The Classic Deluxe Pizza** is the top pizza by number of orders.
* **The Classic Deluxe Pizza** is also the top pizza by quantity sold.
* **The Brie Carre Pizza** is the lowest-performing pizza across revenue, orders, and quantity sold.

### Monthly Revenue

The dashboard tracks revenue across all months and highlights fluctuations in monthly performance.

* Highest monthly revenue: **July – 72.6K**
* Lowest monthly revenue: **February – 65.2K**

### Orders by Day of Week

The dashboard shows that:

* **Thursday** has the highest number of orders — **3,535**
* **Saturday** has the lowest number of orders — **2,624**

### Sales by Category

Pizza sales are distributed across four categories:

* Classic
* Supreme
* Chicken
* Veggie

### Sales by Size

The dashboard also analyzes sales across:

* Large
* Medium
* Small
* X-Large
* XX-Large

The **Large** pizza size contributes the highest share of sales.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `Pizza_Sales_Dashboard.pbix` using **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.
3. Refresh the dataset if required.
4. Use the filters to explore sales performance by month, category, and pizza size.
5. Interact with the visuals to analyze different business metrics.
